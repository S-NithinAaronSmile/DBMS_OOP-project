Project Overview

Problem Statement & Motivation

02 / 10
The Operational Challenge
  • Enterprise HR information is fragmented across distinct entities: employees, departments, managers, and assigned projects.
  • Simple isolated queries fail to answer critical business questions like cross-department staffing and department vacancies.
  • Direct command-line database querying lacks user-friendly access, validation, and object encapsulation for end-users.
The DataForge Solution
  • Robust Relational Core: 4 normalized tables (3NF) maintaining referential integrity across 15 official queries.
  • OOP Layering: Clean separation of concerns with DAO, DTO, and Singleton patterns decoupling SQL from UI.
  • Interactive Console Suite: Fast on-demand query execution, instant batch demo mode, and automated database reseeding.
Key Objective: Bridge Relational Database Management with Clean Object-Oriented Software Design. 15 / 15 Queries Verified
